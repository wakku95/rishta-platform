## 4. Photo Sharing & Strict Privacy Protocol

### `/photo_policy` — Photo Mangne Wale ko Platform Policy Samjhana
> **Respected Member,**  
> RaabtaNow par khandani izzat aur privacy hamari pehli tarjeeh hai. Photos ke hawalay se hamari sakht policy darj zail hai:  
> 1. **Two-Way Exchange:** Photo tabhi share ki jati hai jab dono khandano ki tasweer hamare agent ko mosool ho jaye.  
> 2. **Confidential Watermark:** Tasweer par receiver ke naam ka security watermark lagaya jata hai taake koi misuse na kar sake.  
> 3. **View-Once Delivery:** Tasweer WhatsApp "View Once" (sirf 1 dafa dekhne) par bheji jati hai jisme screenshot ya save karna block hota hai.  
> Baraye meherbani pehle apne candidate ki decent picture share farmayein.

## 7. Common Customer Questions & FAQs

### `/faq_guarantee` — "Kya Rishta Pakka Hone ki Guarantee Hai?"
> **Jee nahi,** rishta aur naseeb Allah Ta'ala ke ikhtiyar mein hai. Hum sirf ba-waqar, verified aur genuine zariya faraham karte hain. Hamari guarantee is baat ki hai ke candidate genuine hai aur dono taraf ki razamandi ke baad hi baat karwayi ja rahi hai.

---

### `/faq_why_fee` — "Aap Sirf Number Dene ke Paise Kyun Le Rahe Hain?"
> **Respected Member,**  
> Hum sirf number nahi de rahe, balki hamare agent ne dono khandano se rabta karke unka background, divorce/job details, aur baahmi razamandi confirm ki hai.  
> Traditional bureaus 15,000–20,000 advance leti hain bina kisi guarantee ke. Hamara idara pehle koi advance nahi leta—jab dono taraf se baat pakki ho jaye, sirf tab agent ki mehnat aur coordination ki mamooli fees li jaati hai.

---

### `/faq_meeting_arrange` — "Kya Aap Ghar Par Meeting Arrange Karwayenge?"
> Pehla marhala phone aur WhatsApp par baahmi baat-cheet ka hota hai. Jab dono khandan basic cheezon par mutma'in ho jayein, toh aap baahmi razamandi se ghar ki meeting ya kisi public family venue par mulaqat tay kar sakte hain.

---

### `/faq_overseas` — "Overseas Pakistanis ke Rishtey Kaise Handle Hote Hain?"
> Overseas candidates ke case mein pehli meeting Zoom / WhatsApp video call par parents ki mojoodgi mein karwayi jati hai. Sath hi Pakistan mein unke muqami rishtedaron ya address ki basic verification share ki jati hai.
